01

Corporate Finance

We help businesses, investors and financial institutions raise money and put structures around it — from debt and equity fundraising to securitisations and large infrastructure financings. We are just as comfortable on a first funding round as on a billion-dollar transaction.

Services

  • Debt & Equity Fundraising
  • Securitisations
  • Infrastructure Financing
  • Deal Structuring
  • Lender & Investor Advisory

02

Tax Advisory & Disputes

Good tax advice starts before the deal is signed, not after the assessment lands. We structure transactions so our clients stay compliant and pay what they should, no more. When a dispute does arise, we take it on — whether that means talking it through with the Kenya Revenue Authority or fighting it at the Tax Appeals Tribunal and in court.

Services

  • Transaction Tax Structuring
  • Tax Compliance
  • Revenue Authority Engagement
  • Tax Appeals Tribunal
  • Tax Litigation

03

Corporate M&A

We advise on mergers, acquisitions and restructurings across East Africa. That covers the whole arc of a deal: how to structure it, what the shareholders' agreement should say, which competition authorities need to clear it, and what to do when a jurisdiction throws up something no one planned for.

Services

  • Mergers & Acquisitions
  • Corporate Restructuring
  • Shareholders' Agreements
  • Competition Clearance
  • Cross-Border Transactions

04

Regulatory & Compliance

Setting up in a new market is rarely just paperwork. We help clients work out the right entity and structure, get licensed, and build compliance frameworks they can actually live with. We deal regularly with the regulators, and those relationships mean we can usually tell you early what will work and what will not.

Services

  • Market Entry & Entity Structuring
  • Licensing
  • Compliance Frameworks
  • Financial Services Regulation
  • Insurance Regulation

05

Commercial Disputes

When things go wrong, we act. We handle commercial litigation, arbitration and mediation — from shareholder fallouts and contracts that were not honoured to employment and land disputes, in the Kenyan courts and out of them. We are candid about the merits from the outset, because knowing when to settle matters as much as knowing when to fight.

Services

  • Commercial Litigation
  • Arbitration
  • Mediation
  • Shareholder Disputes
  • Employment & Land Disputes

06

Technology, Data & Fintech

Technology moves faster than the rules that govern it, and we work at that edge. We advise payment providers, lenders, platforms and other digital businesses on licensing and product design, and we help clients get data protection right: audits, compliance frameworks, and defending complaints when they come. Our team includes published researchers on data protection and intermediary liability, which means we tend to see where the regulation is heading, not just where it stands.

Services

  • Payments & Digital Lending
  • Product & Regulatory Design
  • Data Protection Audits
  • Data Protection Compliance
  • Complaints & Enforcement Defence
